Blinki: One-Stop Coffee Service

A quick UX project demonstrating the design process, with voice assistance as part of the solution.

Role
User and competitor analysis, workflows, wireframes, prototype, UI, style guide
Timeline
2 days
Tools
Adobe XD, Google Slides, Illustrator, Photoshop
Disciplines
UX / UI, VUI, Branding

Overview

This case study centers on Julia, a busy career woman with a young child. She loves coffee and needs a fast way to order from her favorite nearby shop to stay energized.

Problem: waiting in long lines is miserable and skipping her morning coffee can ruin her day. She wants a quick, simple way to order from her favorite cafe or a local shop she wants to support.

Solution: Blinki lets you order coffee in the blink of an eye from any shop nearby.

  • Hands-free ordering by voice assistant
  • One app for any local coffee shop
  • Shows how many orders are ahead of you and estimates pickup time
  • Order from your favorite menu at your favorite shop
  • Instant search for nearby stores

Competitors

Pros: order ahead from any local store; promotions and rewards.

Cons: users must search for stores and menus; no way to know how many orders are ahead; no voice assistant, so the app needs physical interaction.

Persona: Julia

I wish there was an easy way to order a coffee from my favorite store or shop nearby when I need it. I don't have time for long lines.

Goal: a quick, easy order no matter how busy she is or where she is.

  • Long lines just to get a coffee
  • Can't order ahead or plan a pickup
  • Can't easily find other nearby shops
  • Hard to use the screen on the go

Problem and hypothesis

Problem: Julia needs a quick, convenient way to order coffee with an estimated pickup time. She's on the go and her hands are always busy.

Hypothesis: a coffee-network app with ordering from any store, estimated pickup times, scheduled order reminders and a voice interface would save the 10-15 minutes spent waiting in line.

User stories and task flows

Story 1: order from her favorite menu

Check the estimated pickup time before ordering: log in by voice, land on 'My Favorite', view the pickup time, order at my favorite place.

Story 2: order from another nearby store

Find a nearby shop with the fastest pickup: log in by voice, view the nearest alternate shop with similar coffee, place the order, pay with the Blinki card.

Wireframes and prototype

Key features: voice assistant, one-swipe ordering from 'My Favorite', scheduled reminders to place orders, and finding stores by estimated time to pickup.

Style guide

Roasted coffee, orange and light blue stand for energy and a delightful day. Type is Europa and Rubrik from Adobe Fonts: sans serif for a modern, clean look, with Rubrik's rounded corners keeping it lively.
